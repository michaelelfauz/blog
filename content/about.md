## Welcome to my CTF Writeup Blog! 🔐

Hi! I'm **michaelelfauz**, a cybersecurity enthusiast and CTF player. This blog is my collection of Capture The Flag writeups, security research, and learning experiences in the world of cybersecurity.

## About Me

I participate in various CTF competitions, both national and international. My focus areas include:

- 🔓 Cryptography
- 🌐 Web Security
- 🔍 Forensics
- 🔄 Reverse Engineering
- 💻 Binary Exploitation

## Why This Blog?

I created this blog to:

1. **Document my learning journey** - Writing helps solidify my understanding
2. **Share knowledge** - Help others who might be stuck on similar challenges
3. **Build a portfolio** - Showcase my problem-solving skills and technical abilities
4. **Connect with the community** - Learn from others and contribute back

## CTF Competitions

I regularly participate in:

- National CTF competitions (SNICTF, N2L)

- Special events like LastSeenIn2026, Sbnthesis
